🔬 Skill Sets

Design and Simulation
I have extensive experience in multiphysics design and simulation using commercial tools such as COMSOL and Ansys Lumerical. I frequently design photonic and phononic circuit components, cavity structures, and study multiphysics coupling effects — including electro-optic, electromechanical, and optomechanical interactions.
Nanofabrication
I have acquired extensive expertise in nanofabrication for integrated photonic devices, with proficiency in process development and the application of techniques such as lithography, thin-film deposition, reactive-ion etching, surface treatment, and characterization.
Measurement
I have developed proficient optical measurement skills for integrated photonics, including optical and RF signal characterization, filtering, coherent measurements, and feedback control for integrated photonic devices. My work spans both passive and active components, with a focus on nonlinear optical phenomena such as acousto-optics and electro-optics. I also have hands-on experience in generation and stablization Kerr frequency comb.
Programming and Data Analysis
I have strong programming skills in Python and MATLAB, with a focus on data analysis, simulation, and automation. I regularly use these skills for processing experimental data, assisting simulation, and automating measurement workflows.
